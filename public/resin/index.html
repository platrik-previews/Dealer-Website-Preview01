Useful answers

Before you choose your surface.

Every property is different, but these are the questions homeowners ask most often before arranging a survey.

Sometimes. A sound, stable and suitable existing base may allow an overlay, but cracks, movement, drainage and levels must be assessed in person. Where the base is unsuitable, we recommend a full preparation system.

Timescales depend on area, access, groundwork and weather. Straightforward installations may be completed within several working days, while larger projects or full excavations need longer. Your quotation includes a project-specific programme.

The resin-bound surface itself is porous. Whether the complete driveway functions as a permeable system depends on the underlying build-up and site conditions, which we assess during the survey.

Regular sweeping and occasional gentle pressure washing normally keep the surface clean. Spills should be treated promptly, and harsh chemicals or aggressive jet settings should be avoided.

Yes. We bring physical aggregate samples to the survey and help you compare warmer, cooler and darker blends against your brick, render, doors and landscaping. Borders can be subtle, contrasting or removed for a continuous look.
