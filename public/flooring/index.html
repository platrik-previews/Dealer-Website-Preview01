The ACO Flooring approach

The floor earns attention.
The process earns trust.

Good installation makes the invisible work clear before the final material is committed.

◷

One considered sequence
from first conversation to final detail.

01

Room and requirement review

Discuss the room, current floor, preferred look, daily use and any practical concerns.

02

Material direction

Narrow the flooring options around durability, maintenance, moisture, budget and visual direction.

03

Subfloor preparation

Remove unsuitable materials where required, repair defects and create a suitable level base.

04

Fitting and alignment

Install the chosen system with consistent layout, measured cuts and suitable expansion details.

05

Finishing and handover

Resolve trims, thresholds and edges, then explain practical care for the selected floor.
